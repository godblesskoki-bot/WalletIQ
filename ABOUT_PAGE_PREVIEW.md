## 🎯 User Flow

```
1. User on Homepage
   ↓
2. Scrolls to Bitcoin Price
   ↓
3. Sees "About WalletIQ" button
   ↓
4. Hovers (button glows, arrow moves)
   ↓
5. Clicks button
   ↓
6. Page transitions to /about
   ↓
7. About page fades in
   ↓
8. User reads timeline (2026 → today)
   ↓
9. Scrolls through features
   ↓
10. Sees mission statement
   ↓
11. Reads important disclaimers
   ↓
12. Can either:
    → Click "Check Wallet Now" (go to homepage)
    → Click "← Back to Home" (go to homepage)
```
